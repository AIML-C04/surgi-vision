# SurgiVision AI: A Real-Time Intelligent Surgical Video Analytics Platform for Instrument Detection, Tracking, and Surgical Phase Recognition

Minimally invasive surgeries, including laparoscopic and endoscopic procedures, generate continuous video streams that surgeons rely on throughout the operation. During surgery, clinicians must simultaneously monitor surgical instruments, recognize procedural phases, identify unexpected events, and manually document operative details, increasing cognitive workload and the possibility of human error. Existing operating room systems primarily record surgical videos without providing intelligent real-time analysis, while most AI-based surgical video analysis methods are limited to offline processing, individual tasks, or require high computational resources, making them unsuitable for real-time clinical assistance. To address these limitations, this project proposes SurgiVision AI, a deep learning-based computer vision platform for intelligent surgical video analytics. The system aims to automatically detect and track surgical instruments, recognize surgical workflow phases, and generate structured surgical timelines and procedure summaries from laparoscopic videos. As an initial proof of concept, the proposed approach will be validated using short annotated video clips to understand the complete model fine-tuning pipeline, followed by fine-tuning a YOLO-based object detection model using publicly available datasets such as Cholec80 and EndoVis. A web-based dashboard will visualize detected instruments, workflow phases, and analytical reports to support surgeons, surgical trainees, and hospital quality assurance teams. The scope of the project is limited to surgical instrument detection, tracking, workflow phase recognition, and automated report generation, excluding clinical diagnosis, robotic surgery control, and deployment in live operating rooms. The proposed system will be evaluated using Precision, Recall, mAP, tracking accuracy, and inference speed to assess its effectiveness as a research-oriented solution for surgical video understanding, medical education, and healthcare analytics.
